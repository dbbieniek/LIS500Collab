Project Overview

Both group members contributed 100 original photos of clothing items in order to train a Google Teachable Machine image classification model.

The images were given one of two classifications; Summer or Winter.

We were interested in getting a better understanding of how AI systems recognize size, shape, and color, and thinking about how these factors can propagate biases, stereotypes, and inequality within programs.
